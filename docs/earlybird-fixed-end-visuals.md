# EarlyBird 固定文末尾图

每篇草稿在“参考资料”之后使用同一组两张图。这两张是栏目视觉不是事实证据；具体内容的理解仍依赖正文中的 X 截图、官方原图和视频关键帧。

生成后保存到：`data/earlybird/media/fixed-end/`

## 1. `earlybird-endcard-signal.png`

用途：让读者在文章收束时感知到“持续追踪官方信息”，不做关注引导或事实断言。

提示词：

```
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: fixed WeChat article end card for EarlyBird Pulse
Primary request: a quiet editorial visual about carefully tracking reliable AI signals over time
Scene/backdrop: a deep graphite field with a restrained constellation of warm gold signal points connected by thin, precise lines; a subtle horizon suggests ongoing observation
Style/medium: premium technology editorial illustration, clean print-like composition, gentle depth, no cyberpunk excess
Composition/framing: wide 16:9, centered visual rhythm, generous negative space
Lighting/mood: calm, credible, reflective
Color palette: graphite black, ivory, restrained warm gold, a tiny hint of muted teal
Constraints: no readable text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no QR code, no buttons, no people, no watermark
```

## 2. `earlybird-endcard-observe.png`

用途：将阅读的注意力收回“事件还在发展”，与第一张共同组成一组尾图。

提示词：

```
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: fixed WeChat article end card for EarlyBird Pulse
Primary request: an elegant editorial visual about observing how a technology story develops beyond the first announcement
Scene/backdrop: layered translucent graphite panels opening toward a soft distant glow, with a few measured warm-gold paths continuing beyond the visible frame
Style/medium: refined AI technology magazine illustration, minimal, tactile paper-and-glass textures, sophisticated but restrained
Composition/framing: wide 16:9, lower visual density than a hero image, deliberate quiet ending
Lighting/mood: thoughtful, clear, forward-looking
Color palette: deep graphite, soft off-white, muted warm gold, extremely subtle teal accent
Constraints: no readable text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no QR code, no call-to-action, no people, no watermark
```

不使用参考图中的第三方品牌名称、二维码、帐号信息或互动诱导。可以借鉴其“深色底、暖光点、居中视觉重心”的氛围，但尾图保持 EarlyBird 自身的克制科技编辑风格。
